# Cinematic arena imagery

The original Signature logo, launcher and jersey atlas are unchanged.

Two original decorative arena images were generated for this owner-authorized
native redesign on November 15, 2026. They are not photographs of the user,
roster members, a named athlete, or an actual team.

| Android resource | Purpose | Origin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `court_arena_player.webp` | Welcome / atmospheric Court background | Original generated anonymous adult athlete, plain unbranded kit, face turned away, no name or number |
| `court_arena_hoop.webp` | Train / Team atmosphere | Original generated empty indoor basketball arena and hoop |

No third-party stock photograph, team insignia or illustrative reference branding
was copied. Generation prompts prohibited text, numbers, logos and recognizable
public figures. Compressed WebP resources are bundled for offline use; no remote
image service is called by the app. High-resolution authoring outputs remain in
the ignored `artifacts/cinematic-assets/` directory, not Git.

Player identity is rendered separately in Compose from saved player state and
the existing approved jersey artwork. Unset avatars use a neutral native icon.
Neither decorative image is represented as a real player avatar.
