# Historical drill artwork proposal

This four-image proposal is superseded. Its referenced ZIP and WebP files were not part
of the repository, so they are not used by the app.

The current complete offline library is tracked under
`app/src/main/assets/drill_art/`. The deterministic ID-to-asset mapping and per-drill
coverage, comparison decisions, dimensions, sizes, and verification status are in
[DRILL_ART_COVERAGE.md](DRILL_ART_COVERAGE.md) and `manifest.json`.
