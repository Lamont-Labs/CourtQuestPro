# CourtQuest Pro privacy policy

**Owner review before publication.** Lamont Labs must confirm the public support email, effective date, publishing entity, and publicly accessible policy URL before submitting this policy through Google Play.

## Information on your device

CourtQuest Pro uses the name and jersey details you enter, practice settings, drill and workout records, local scores, and progress to personalize your experience. A name is optional. Use a nickname if you prefer. The app stores these records in its private storage on your Android device and excludes them from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

## Online services and sharing

CourtQuest Pro does not require an account. Lamont Labs does not receive your player profile, saved drill results, jersey, scores, or progress from this app. The app has no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting service operated by Lamont Labs. If you choose a demonstration link, the app opens a basketball-drill search in a separate browser or YouTube. The receiving service may process the search and information about your use under its own privacy policy. CourtQuest Pro does not put your saved player name, custom drill titles, jersey settings, or results in the search.

Your installation and update through Google Play may involve Google's account and device information under Google's own terms. That processing occurs through the store.

## Retention and removal

Local data stays in CourtQuest Pro until you update or remove it in the app, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes the app's local records. A routine compatible Android update retains the existing records.

## Contact

Questions about this policy should go to Lamont Labs through the support contact shown on the official CourtQuest Pro Google Play listing when the app is published.
